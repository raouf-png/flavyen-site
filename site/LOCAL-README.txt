FLAVYEN DUPONT — LOCAL PSP / XMB REFINEMENT

Open index.html directly, or serve REALISATEUR_SITE locally and visit /site/.
Film videos resolve from ../FLAVYEN_DUPONT/; keep that folder beside site/.
No remote fonts, scripts, image requests, uploads or publishing.
Vimeo, Instagram and email open only when a visitor explicitly chooses them.

The selected film uses the entire screen, with no image cropping.

Escape closes playback. Each category remembers its selected film.
