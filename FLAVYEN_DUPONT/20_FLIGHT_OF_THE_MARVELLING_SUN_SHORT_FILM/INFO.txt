TEXTE DE LA PAGE

FLIGHT OF THE MARVELLING SUN.
Once upon a time in Paris, a young couple
start to live some strange and mysterious event.

CONTEXT
After a first short film dealing with the loss of oneself in love as a young adult, I wanted to explore, in the same coherent universe, the loss of oneself as a person following a tragic event.
